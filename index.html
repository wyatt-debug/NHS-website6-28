Biomentum

Certified product

Aminos-N

Stress-proof your yields with premium plant nutrition

Hog manure and more: the power of the Pit Puck

Traditional manure management is an uphill battle against crusting, harsh odors, and critical nutrient loss. Our biological approach shifts the focus from simple waste storage to active nutrient optimization — turning your barn's waste into a high-value fertilizer asset. Drop these all-natural, OMRI listed pucks directly into your facility's waste system and a focused microbial digestion cascade goes to work.

Nutrient stabilization

Keep your fertilizer value in the pit, not in the air

The biology locks vital nutrients into a stable, plant-available liquid state, preventing valuable fertilizer elements from escaping into the atmosphere as gas. What you store stays usable when it's time to apply it to your fields.

Ammonia and odor reduction

A healthier barn for your pigs and your crew

By stabilizing nitrogen, you drastically eliminate the harsh, ambient ammonia smells that lead to sick pigs and poor working conditions. The result is a barn that's easier to breathe in and better for your animals' performance.

Facility longevity

Protect the investment you've built

Lowering corrosive ammonia gas levels protects your investment, preventing long-term structural and equipment deterioration inside the barn. Less corrosion means fewer repairs and a longer service life for your facility.

Waste liquefaction

Break down sludge, crust, and methane hazards

These specialized microbes aggressively accelerate decomposition to actively reduce heavy bottom sludge, eliminate thick crusting blockages, and prevent foaming methane hazards — keeping your pit flowing and safe.

Ponds and lakes: organic Pond Pucks

Traditional aquatic management relies on harsh "chemical shock" treatments that deliver only temporary visual fixes while leaving underlying issues untouched. Our approach focuses on whole-ecosystem restoration — addressing the root causes of water degradation to create self-regulating, clear, and resilient water bodies. Drop these all-organic, enzyme-driven pucks straight into your target waters and a continuous biological cleanup cascade begins.

Natural algae prevention

Starve blooms at the source

Toxic algae blooms are aggressively driven by excess nitrogen and phosphorus. These specialized microbes prevent blooms by outcompeting algae for those vital nutrients, locking them safely into microbial biomass instead.

Breaking the nutrient loop

Digest the muck that feeds the problem

Even when external runoff is reduced, heavy bottom muck and sludge act as a hidden reservoir that constantly feeds algae. Our biological solution breaks this recycling loop by digesting the muck at the bottom of the column.

Stable oxygen levels

Protect your fish while the water clears

Sudden chemical algaecides cause rapid plant die-offs that lead to dangerous oxygen depletion and recurring blooms. Our microbes suppress unwanted growth gradually, protecting fish and supporting healthier, balanced aquatic life.

Enhanced decomposition

Clearer water, permanently lower nutrient loading

Boosting microbial diversity dramatically speeds up the natural breakdown of organic matter, improving water clarity and permanently reducing the internal nutrient loading that fuels future blooms.
