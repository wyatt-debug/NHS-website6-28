Biomentum

Certified product

Transform your acreage by activating the natural biological engine right beneath your feet. Biomentum is a cutting edge microbial formula that introduces a highly targeted blend of beneficial bacteria alongside mycorrhizal and saprophytic fungi. By re-establishing this vital ecosystem, it triggers the plant's natural defenses and breaks down stubborn, locked up minerals into forms your crops can actually digest. Whether you are looking to suppress aggressive diseases like white mold, treat manure, or optimize your forage and hay quality, this highly versatile biological tool helps you grow more while spending less on traditional chemical inputs.

ABC Soil Health

ABC Soil Health combines Aminos-N, Biomentum, and humic acid so nutrients stay in the root zone, native microbes get a boost, and early-season vigor has more to draw on.

ABC Soil Health Organic

The organic blend is liquid fish, humic acid, and Biomentum. It loosens tight soils, holds more water and nutrients, and feeds the biology under an organic program.

Aminos-N

Aminos-N supplies 18 L-form amino acids and 16 trace minerals as organic nitrogen the plant can use during heat, drought, and other stress.

Pit Pucks

Drop the pucks into the manure system.

Nutrient stabilization

Nutrients stay in a stable, plant-available liquid instead of leaving the pit as gas.

Ammonia and odor reduction

Less ammonia in the air means an easier barn for the pigs and the crew.

Facility longevity

Less corrosive gas means less wear on the barn and the equipment in it.

Waste liquefaction

The microbes break down bottom sludge, crust, and foaming methane so the pit keeps moving.

Pond Pucks

Drop the pucks in the water.

Natural algae prevention

The microbes take up excess nitrogen and phosphorus so algae has less to bloom on.

Breaking the nutrient loop

Bottom muck is digested so it stops feeding the next bloom.

Stable oxygen levels

Growth is suppressed gradually, so fish are not hit with a sudden oxygen drop.

Enhanced decomposition

Faster breakdown of organic matter clears the water and lowers the nutrients stored in it.

About us

Wyatt Streit

Co-founder

Born and raised on a farm in Bode, Iowa, with family roots tied to the state's earliest farming settlements, Wyatt has a lifelong understanding of the challenges facing modern growers. A natural entrepreneur with a knack for creative problem solving, he co-founded Natural Heartland Solutions while studying at Iowa State University. As the driving force behind the brand's distribution, Wyatt's mission goes far beyond moving product. He brings fresh, innovative thinking to the field, working directly with the agricultural community to educate growers on manure optimization, regulatory compliance, and sustainable soil health. Wyatt combines entrepreneurial grit, a passion for learning, and a commitment to delivering the forward thinking solutions Iowa farmers need to succeed.

Evan Deal

Co-founder

Born and raised on a small family farm in Algona, Iowa, Evan's deep connection to the land has shaped his entire life and career. Today, he continues to work the ground alongside his father, maintaining operations of the family farm. He co-founded this company while actively pursuing his higher education at Iowa State University and serving his country in the Iowa Air National Guard. Driven by a genuine passion for structural rural sustainability, Evan helped implement our high efficiency biological formulas to give producers a proven, effective alternative to traditional heavy synthetics. Beyond the field, his dedication to the community extends into a deep engagement with Iowa and federal politics, keeping him at the forefront of the policies affecting rural America.
